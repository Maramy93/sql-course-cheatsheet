# Sources and provenance

This tutorial was written in original language around a newly created shop dataset. Supplied course notes and images were inspected to identify topic coverage and learning context; their prose, screenshots, diagrams, exercises, and DVD-rental database were not reproduced. This guide is independently organized and adds interview topics. It does not claim to summarize unseen lecture details.

Original illustrations: `assets/learning-map.svg`, `assets/shop-schema.svg`, `assets/join-rows.svg`, and `assets/query-order.svg`, plus Mermaid diagrams in chapters. SVGs were drawn programmatically for this guide; all examples/data are fictional. Examples and technical explanations were checked against the official references below. No logos or course branding are included.
